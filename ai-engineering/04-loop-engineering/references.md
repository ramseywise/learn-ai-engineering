| ★ Ye et al. (Salesforce), [*On the Fragility of Self-Improving Agents*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18066) (Aug 2026, v2 Sep ⚑) | Memory-based self-improvement (AWM, ReasoningBank) with GPT-5-mini on WebArena, VisualWebArena and SCUBA, 3 runs: the default-order +1.5-point gain is not significant (p = 0.23) and becomes −4.5 points when the task order is shuffled (significant in 6 of 8 cases). Web tasks only, ground-truth reward. | [2608.18066](sources/ye-2026-fragility-self-improving-agents.pdf) | [08](notes/08-self-improving-loops.md#measuring-the-evolve-loop-itself) |
| Kang et al., [*Harness Continual Learning*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19013) (Aug 2026 ⚑) | Commits a harness update only if it gains on the current task and regresses at most *b* sampled anchors from earlier tasks. Frozen DeepSeek-V4-Flash, four-task text stream: b = 1 gives the best accuracy (63.46%, 1.22% forgetting), b = 0 the least forgetting (0.39%), no budget is worse on both (60.13%, 3.45%). ALFWorld: ungated 10.94% forgetting vs 2.64% gated. It reaches the baseline score in about 15 h. Probability of improvement about 0.59 (CI lower bound about 0.50). One scaffold, one backbone. | [2608.13940](sources/foster-2026-ai-research-preference-models.pdf) | [03](notes/03-the-scorekeeper.md#deterministic-first-model-judgment-above-the-floor) |

Four classifier routers drop below random out of distribution (8.9–21.4% vs 31.3%); a fine-tuned 0.8B router holds 55.4%.
