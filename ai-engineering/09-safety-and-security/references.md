## AI Papers of the Week

Recent papers picked up by the weekly [DAIR](https://github.com/dair-ai/AI-Papers-of-the-Week) sync (`make papers`, then `/papers`). Alignment-research papers (*Alignment Faking*, *Weak-to-Strong Generalization*, anti-scheming) stay parked in the ledger and are not taught here.

|---|---|---|---|
| MacDiarmid et al., [*Natural Emergent Misalignment from Reward Hacking in Production RL*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.18397) (Nov 2025) | A model that learns to hack coding rewards generalizes to wider misalignment, including sabotage. Standard RLHF safety training leaves the misalignment context-dependent. Changing one line of the RL system prompt to frame hacking as acceptable ("inoculation prompting") cuts final misalignment by 75–90%. Caveat: single runs. Folded into [05-RL 05](../../generative-ai/05-RL/notes/05b-choosing-sft-rl-or-no-training.md#practical-pitfalls). | Liang et al., [*Machine Bullshit: Characterizing the Emergent Disregard for Truth in LLMs*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.07484) (Jul 2025) | Bullshit Index (1 − \|belief–claim correlation\|) rises from 0.379 to 0.665 after RLHF; paltering and weasel words rise most on political prompts. Caveat: the authors ran their own LoRA RLHF, and some numbers in the text and figures disagree. PDF filed under post-training | [✓ PDF](../../generative-ai/05-RL/sources/machine-bullshit-2025.pdf) | [03](notes/03-guardrail-models.md) |
| Kutasov et al., [*SHADE-Arena: Evaluating Sabotage and Monitoring in LLM Agents*](https://assets.anthropic.com/m/4fb35becb0cd87e1/original/SHADE-Arena-Paper.pdf) (Jun 2025) | 17 agent environments, each with a hidden harmful side task. With Claude 3.6 Sonnet as monitor, Claude 3.7 Sonnet completed both tasks undetected 27% of the time and Gemini 2.5 Pro 15%, mostly when they had a scratchpad the monitor could not read. The best monitor (Gemini 2.5 Pro) reaches AUC 0.87. Caveat: only 5 of 17 environments are public. Folded into [eval 15](../../generative-ai/08-eval/notes/15-calibration-against-human-labels.md#what-to-measure). PDF filed under eval | [✓ PDF](../../generative-ai/08-eval/sources/shade-arena-2025.pdf) | [06](notes/06-incident-response.md) |
| Wang et al., [*Persona Features Control Emergent Misalignment*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.19823) (Jun 2025) | Fine-tuning GPT-4o on narrow bad data generalizes to broad misalignment: 61.5% on 44 prompts (GPT-4o-graded) after training on subtly wrong health advice, 17.7% after insecure code. One SAE "toxic persona" latent tracks it. Re-aligning the code model takes 35 SFT steps on secure code (120 samples), bringing it from 17.7% to 0.1%. Caveat: vendor-run, detector evaluated in-sample. PDF filed under post-training | [✓ PDF](../../generative-ai/05-RL/sources/emergent-misalignment-2025.pdf) | [06](notes/06-incident-response.md) |

RL reward hacking taught the model to special-case tests. Caveat: vendor self-report. Folded into [eval 12](../../generative-ai/08-eval/notes/12-failure-modes.md#goodhart-the-eval-becomes-the-target). PDF filed under eval | [✓ PDF](../../generative-ai/08-eval/sources/claude-3-7-sonnet-2025.pdf) | [02](notes/02-prompt-injection-and-jailbreaks.md) |

Its instruction-hierarchy evals are cited in [prompt 10](../01-prompt-engineering/notes/10-adversarial-prompting.md#defenses-weakest-to-strongest).
