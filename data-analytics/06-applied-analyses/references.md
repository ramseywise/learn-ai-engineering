# Applied analyses: references

Reading for this pillar. ★ marks a reading that changed a claim in the notes. Each row says how far it was read, because not every book was to hand. Fernhill's data is synthetic, generated by `practice/retail.py`, so any Fernhill number is ours and not from these sources.

## Books

| Book | Why read it | Notes | How it was used |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hyndman & Athanasopoulos, [*Forecasting: Principles and Practice*, 3rd ed.](https://otexts.com/fpp3/) (OTexts, 2021) | The standard free forecasting text, with the benchmark methods, accuracy measures and cross-validation that the chapter is built on | [01](notes/01-forecasting.md) | Sections 5.2, 5.8, 5.10 and 13.1 and the harmonic regression section read; basis of the method list |
| Hyndman, Athanasopoulos, Garza, Challu, Mergenthaler & Olivares, [*Forecasting: Principles and Practice, the Pythonic Way*](https://otexts.com/fpppy/) (OTexts) | The same ideas with Python code | [01](notes/01-forecasting.md) | Listed for readers working in Python; not read in full, no claims taken from it |
| Ord, Fildes & Kourentzes, *Principles of Business Forecasting*, 2nd ed. (Wessex) | A business-facing treatment of forecasting as part of planning | [01](notes/01-forecasting.md) | Further reading; not read, no claims taken from it |
| Siddiqi, *Intelligent Credit Scoring*, 2nd ed. (Wiley, 2017, ISBN 9781119282297) | The practitioner's book on scorecards | [02](notes/02-credit-scoring.md) | Not read. The IV bands are reported second-hand through the MWSUG paper below |
| Thomas, Crook & Edelman, [*Credit Scoring and Its Applications*, 2nd ed.](https://doi.org/10.1137/1.9781611974560) (SIAM, 2017) | The more mathematical treatment of the same subject | [02](notes/02-credit-scoring.md) | Further reading; record checked, not read, no claims taken from it |

| Bickel, Hammel & O'Connell, [Sex Bias in Graduate Admissions: Data from Berkeley](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.187.4175.398) (Science, 1975) | The classic example of Simpson's paradox | [04](notes/04-funnel-and-conversion.md) | Record checked; cited for the example, as in [pillar 03](../03-experimentation-and-causality/references.md) |

| McInnes, Healy & Astels, [hdbscan: Hierarchical density based clustering](https://doi.org/10.21105/joss.00205) (Journal of Open Source Software, 2017; [PDF](../sources/mcinnes-2017-hdbscan-joss.pdf)) | The short description of HDBSCAN: DBSCAN over varying epsilon, integrated to find the clusters that persist | [05](notes/05-segmentation-and-rfm.md#case-segmenting-b2b-customers-by-behaviour) | Read in full |
