### Judges and graders

| | Paper | arXiv | Chapters |
|---|---|---|---|
| ★ | Zheng et al., *Judging LLM-as-a-Judge with MT-Bench and Chatbot Arena* (NeurIPS 2023 Datasets & Benchmarks): GPT-4 matched human experts' pairwise preferences about 85% of the time (ties excluded), about the human–human rate. Position bias was the solid finding: under answer-swapping GPT-4 kept its verdict 65% of the time, Claude-v1 23.8%. Verbosity bias was also shown; the authors call their self-preference data too limited to establish it (GPT-3.5 did not favour itself). ### Agents

| | Chen et al., *Evaluating Large Language Models Trained on Code* (Codex, 2021 ⚑): the unbiased pass@k estimator (n = 200 samples, k ≤ 100).
