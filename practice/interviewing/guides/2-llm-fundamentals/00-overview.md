# Pillar 2 — LLM Fundamentals (transformers → training → prompting)

How large language models actually work: tokens, attention, the training pipeline
(pretraining → SFT → preference alignment), and how to talk to them. This is the pillar
where you build a tiny GPT with your own hands — after that, nothing about LLMs is magic.

## Learn it in the curriculum

The teaching lives in the chapters. Work them in this order, then use the [interview guide](interview-guide.md) as the recall sheet.

| Chapter | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [NLP](../../../../data-science/03-nlp/README.md) | What came before LLMs, and why it still gets asked |
| [LLM fundamentals](../../../../generative-ai/01-llm-fundamentals/README.md) | Tokens, attention, training, decoding, fine-tuning |
| [LLMs from scratch](../../../../generative-ai/09-llms-from-scratch/README.md) | Micrograd, makemore and a tiny GPT by hand |
| [RL and alignment](../../../../generative-ai/05-RL/README.md) | RLHF, preference optimisation, constitutional AI |
| [Prompt engineering](../../../../ai-engineering/01-prompt-engineering/README.md) | Prompting techniques and how to evaluate them |
