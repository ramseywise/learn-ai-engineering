# Pillar 10 — Product & Delivery (business sense, end-to-end, nonprofit lens)

The last mile: connecting technical work to outcomes — framing problems, ROI arithmetic,
stakeholder translation, and bringing projects end to end, especially in mission-driven
organizations. This pillar is the seed of the future book outline (end-to-end projects in
the nonprofit sector) and the differentiator for FDE/DS roles.
